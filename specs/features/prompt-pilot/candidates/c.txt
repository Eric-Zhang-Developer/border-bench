Draw the land borders and coastline of {target} from memory.

Rules:
- Draw land only. Each separate piece of territory (mainland, island, exclave) is its own polygon. Do not enclose sea.
- Leave out overseas dependencies and Antarctic claims.
- Use at most 400 points in total.
- Your drawing is scored by overlap: the area it shares with the true territory, divided by the total area covered by either. Missing land and extra land both cost you.
- Give your best attempt even if you are unsure. An approximate drawing is expected.

Format:
- You may reason first. End with one ```geojson block; only that block is scored.
- The block holds a single GeoJSON MultiPolygon:
  {"type": "MultiPolygon", "coordinates": [[[[lon, lat], [lon, lat], ...]], [[[lon, lat], ...]]]}
- Longitude first, then latitude, in decimal degrees with at most 2 decimal places. West and south are negative.
