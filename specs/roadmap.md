# Roadmap

## Where this is going
A public benchmark where anyone can watch AI models get better at drawing borders from memory, modern and
historical, with a paper that defends the method. The MVP proves the picture is worth looking at. v1 adds the rigor
and the harder history. The boss fight (a whole patchwork of states) comes last.

## Milestones
- **mvp**: six strips on one page, every served Claude model, one frozen prompt, overlap % under every drawing. True
  when the maintainer looks at the strips and can say whether they show progress.

## After the MVP (not scheduled)
- More providers and older or smaller models, if the strips look flat.
- Average miss in km, oracle ceiling, disputed-area masks, the foil-region history score.
- A larger history tier from CShapes (1886 onward) for statistical power.
- Partition targets: German Confederation 1820 first, then Africa 1914, then the Holy Roman Empire.
- A text question per history target, to compare what a model says with what it draws.
- The arXiv paper.

## Dates that are fixed
- **30 Nov 2026: Claude Sonnet 4.5 retires.** Its answers are archived, and the prompt is frozen, before then.

## Not doing
- Monetization, accounts, user-generated content.
